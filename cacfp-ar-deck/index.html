About this deck. Written from the sponsor's seat: each card covers what a state agency reviewer probes about a sponsor's own operation under 7 CFR Part 226, plus FNS Instruction 113-1 (civil rights) and 2 CFR Part 200 (procurement and cost principles). As of June 1, 2026, FNS is the Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA), so newer source documents may carry the new name. This is a study aid — it is not a substitute for your state agency's official review guidance and not a guarantee of any review outcome. Confirm every item against your FNA regional office's guidance, the current eCFR, and your state agency's own procedures before relying on it in a review.
